TexTAR: Textual Attribute Recognition in Multi-domain and Multi-lingual Document Images
Rohan Kumar, Jyothi Swaroopa Jinka and Ravi Kiran Saravadevabhatla.
International Conference on Document Analysis and Recognition (ICDAR) 2025, Wuhan, China
Oral Presentation · IAPR Best Student Paper Award

[arXiv]

Introduced TexTAR, a multi-task, context-aware Transformer for recognizing textual attributes (bold, italic, underline, strikeout) in noisy, multilingual documents. It uses a data selection pipeline to improve context awareness and a 2D RoPE-style mechanism to incorporate input context. Also released MMTAD, a diverse multilingual, multi-domain dataset annotated with text attributes across real-world documents such as legal records, notices, and textbooks. TexTAR achieves state-of-the-art performance, with a 94% F1 score on MMTAD.
